# Design reference set

The site should feel like a small research institution with a public record, not a founder landing page or a SaaS product catalogue.

- [Other Internet](https://otherinter.net/) — restrained institute identity; links read as citations and invitations, not buttons.
- [The New Institute](https://thenew.institute/en) — disciplined grid, editorial hierarchy, and institutional documentation.
- [Future Observatory](https://futureobservatory.org/) — research programmes presented as authored objects rather than feature cards.
- [Works in Progress](https://worksinprogress.co/) — long-form readability, durable archives, and strong essay indexing.
- Lauris site, commit `5b4eda0` — compact project ledger, Insrt product lineage, quiet accent rules, and fast scanability.

Applied rules:

1. One 150px rail and one 54px gutter govern every institutional section.
2. Controls are underlined editorial links; outlined or filled generic CTA rectangles are avoided.
3. Research work is a four-object catalogue: Statebook and BizHedge are wide features; SLAM and Hedgebook form the paired middle row.
4. Proof comes from the live artifact and concise copy; decorative scale captions are omitted.
5. Microtype stays readable; labels are supporting structure, never grey decoration.
6. Motion explains a system once. It does not compete with screenshots or delay the project inventory.
7. The state-space projections describe reusable operations — boundary, equivalence, correspondence, and residual — rather than individual products.
